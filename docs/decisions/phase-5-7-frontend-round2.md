# Phases 5–7 — Frontend: gate fixes (round 2)

Date: 2026-10-06 · Scope: `frontend/` (one frontend agent) · Contract: `docs/initial-assessment.md` revision 2

Gate review round 2 listed no failing gate items and 11 findings. Every finding was checked against the code and, where it is about layout, measured in a real browser first; all 11 were real. While this round ran, the backend fixer regenerated the contract snapshot and handed three changes to the frontend owner (B57-70, B57-71, B57-72, see the end of `phase-5-7-backend.md`); they are done here too (FY-12), because the snapshot change turned `api.contract.test.ts` red and the other two change what the server does. Where a fix differs from the suggestion, the entry says so and why. Each entry: **decision** — reason. Numbering: FY-n. Entries of earlier files that these fixes change carry an *(Amended in FY-n)* note.

## Reception

FY-1. **"Check out" never takes the place of "Check in" (finding 1; amends FX-19, FR5-14).** — The result's action row has two groups, marked `data-action-group`: `end`, where a check-in sits (the right from `sm`, a full-width bar at the bottom on phones), and `start` (the left; on phones a row whose buttons share the width). ALREADY_CHECKED_IN now puts "Check out" (outlined, `lg`, no longer `xl`) in `start` beside "Scan next" and leaves `end` empty. It still takes focus when the result appears, so Enter checks out as §10 requires; the under-5-minutes "Check out…" (with confirmation) sits there too. Measured in Chromium with the result on screen: at 1440 "Check in" is at x = 1234 and "Check out" at x = 985; at 820 (touch) 622 against 199; at 390 "Check in" is the full-width bottom bar, "Check out" the right half of the row with "Scan next". FX-19's "the position stays the same for muscle memory" is withdrawn: muscle memory is what the rule guards against. `ReceptionScanner.test.tsx` asserts the group of each action and that `end` is absent.

FY-2. **Scan warnings are worded from the visit's times (finding 9).** — `scanNotices(outcome, now, timeZone)` (pure, `scanOutcome.ts`):
   - `OUTSIDE_SCHEDULED_TIME` before the scheduled start: "Early: scheduled 11:35–13:05 (starts in 10m)", info tone; after the scheduled end: "Late: scheduled 09:00–10:15 (ended 25m ago)", warning tone. The app's duration style ("10m") is used rather than "10 min".
   - The server's own text stays when this clock puts the visitor inside the scheduled time (the server judged with its clock) and for any code the client does not know.
   - ALREADY_CHECKED_IN shows `InsideFlagBadges` ("Overdue · due out 10:15", "Previous day") beside the status badge, and `OVERSTAYED`'s generic sentence is left out.
   - The live region reads a fixed form ("Early: scheduled 11:35–13:05.", the flag labels), so it does not speak again every minute; `scanAnnouncement` takes the time zone for this.
   - Kit: `Alert` renders `data-tone`, so a test can pin the tone.

FY-3. **One rule for the action of a list row (finding 2; amends FR5-21, FR5-24).** — `rowAction(visit, now)` in `visitActions.ts` (unit-tested) returns `check-out`, `manual`, `override`, `override-in-menu` (the pass is not valid yet: the audited exception waits in the row's menu) or `walk-in` (the server will not check the visit in). `VisitRowAction` renders the button and `ActionVisitRow` (`VisitRowAction.tsx`) composes `VisitRow` with it, the menu item and the "Check-in not available" badge; Expected and "Find a visitor" both use it. MANUAL is "Verify ID & check in" in both, and an early visitor's "Check in anyway…" is in the menu in both. `TodayVisit` is built on `VisitRow`, which gained `showVisitor`: the visitor is named above their visits in the search, so the row starts with its badges (the arrival state for a scheduled visit; the status and inside flags for a visitor inside) and keeps the time, host, phone, department and purpose. Because a menu alone is easy to miss there, the search adds one note when such a visit is shown ("…can be checked in anyway from the visit's “More actions” menu"), as it already does for late arrivals. `defaultCheckOutAt` is now `new Date(bulkCheckOutAt(visit, now)).toISOString()`.

FY-4. **"Find a visitor" lists the actionable visitors first (finding 6).** — `orderSearchResults` (pure, tested) sorts the page on the client: visitors with someone inside first, then visitors expected today by start time, then the rest in the server's order; each visitor's own visits follow the same order. "Show all" applies after sorting. A visitor without a visit today shows "No visit to check in or out today." first and "Register walk-in" after it as a ghost action. Measured with "chen" (five matches, one actionable): Ingrid Chen is first at 1440 and at 390.

FY-5. **A shorter walk-in form (finding 7; amends FR5-26).**
   - **How long the visitor stays** is a quick pick: "30 min", "1 hour" (default), "2 hours", "Until 17:00", "Other…". The end is computed when the form is sent (`walkInExpectedEnd(values, now, timeZone)`), not when it was opened, and the group's hint reads it back: "Leaves about 10:10 if checked in now." The date and time fields appear only for "Other…", starting at the end picked so far (`withWalkInStay`). A server message on `expected_end` shows on the visible control.
   - *"Until 17:00"* is `OFFICE_DAY_END`, company time, offered while at least 30 minutes of it are left (and kept while chosen; a choice that has passed is refused on sending). The contract has no opening-hours setting; a site whose day ends otherwise uses "Other…". Labels are "1 hour"/"2 hours" rather than "1 h"/"2 h", which read better aloud.
   - **A new visitor's** optional Company, Phone and Email wait behind "Add contact details (optional)"; opening it moves focus to Company. The fields stay open once anything is typed in them, so a server error on one is never hidden.
   - **The action bar is sticky at the bottom on every pointer**, edge to edge, with the safe-area inset. *Deviation:* the finding asked for touch screens only, but at 1440 × 900 the shortened form (1,070 px) still ran past the fold. When the form fits, the bar sits under it. "Clear form" is a ghost button at the far left, the primary at the far right; the order is still safe first, primary last.
   - **Reasons** use the new `RadioGroup` option `layout="grid"`: equal segments, two per row on phones, three from `sm`.
   - Measured in Chromium: "Check in walk-in" ends at 888 px at 1440 × 900, 1,168 px at 820 × 1180 and 832 px at 390 × 844, so it is on screen without scrolling everywhere (before: 1,270 px and 1,336 px forms, the button below the fold). The phone page is about 1,480 px (was about 1,800).

FY-6. **Quick reasons for "Check in anyway" (finding 8).** — New kit component `SuggestionChips`: toggle buttons that fill a free-text field with a common answer, which stays editable; the chip matching the field's text is pressed (check mark, `aria-pressed`). `overrideReasonSuggestions(visit, now)` offers the arrival that applies ("Arrived early – host confirmed" while the pass is not valid yet, otherwise "Arrived late – host confirmed") and "Did not receive the pass"; "Other" clears a picked text and focuses the field. *Deviation:* the arrival that does not apply is left out rather than shown, so it cannot be picked by mistake. The dialog now opens on the first chip instead of the text field, so a tablet's on-screen keyboard stays closed. The 1–300 character rule and the server's field errors are unchanged; the empty-reason message now reads "Choose a common reason, or enter why the visitor is checked in anyway."

## History, users, reports

FY-7. **The visit history fits its card (finding 3; amends FX-30, F57A-9).** — Measured before: a 1,393 px table in boxes of 702, 958 and 1,118 px (1024, 1280, 1440). Now:
   - From `xl` the table has a fixed layout: Status 10.75 rem (the "Corrected check-out" badge, 147 px), Date 8.25 rem, Attendance 10 rem, Visitor 18 % and Host 16 % (both wrap), Purpose the rest, cut to one line with its full text as the title. Below `2xl` an `xl` table's cells are `px-3` (new `headerCell` in the stack config keeps header and body aligned). The department is a muted second line under the host, and attendance is two lines, "In 08:49 · Out 09:51" and "1h 2m" (`attendanceLines`, next to `formatAttendance`). The 2xl-only check-in/check-out/duration columns are gone.
   - *Deviation:* **below `xl` the history is the two-per-row card list** (new `stackBelow="xl"` in the kit `Table`), not a table at 1024. There the box is 702 px; status (147 px badge), date (103 px) and attendance (about 136 px) with their padding take about 476 px, leaving about 226 px for visitor, host and purpose, less than the finding's own 11 rem visitor minimum. Cards keep every fact readable; a 1280 px laptop and wider get the table.
   - After: the wrapper's scroll width equals its width at 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1600, with no page overflow; Purpose gets 168 px at 1280 and 274 px at 1440. A fixed layout cannot overflow, so no scroll shadow is needed.
   - `e2e/history-layout.spec.ts` (Playwright, mocked API, desktop project) asserts `scrollWidth === clientWidth` and that the attendance lies inside the card at 1024, 1280 and 1440 with the widest values. Run against the old table it fails at all three widths (1,402 px); it passes now.

FY-8. **Users on tablets and at 1024 (finding 4; amends F57A-16).** — The list stacks below `lg` (two cards per row from `md`). Measured at 1024, the six-column table was 764 px in 702 even then, so the row now has four columns: the name cell holds the name with its status beside it (an inactive account's name is muted and keeps the "Inactive" badge; "Temporary password" sits there too), the email, and "Last sign-in Yesterday, 23:13" / "Never signed in". *Deviation:* the "Active" badge is gone; only exceptions are flagged, and the Status filter remains. The table is 702 px at 1024. **Kit fix found on the way:** the visually hidden "Actions" header (absolutely positioned `sr-only` text) escaped the table's scroll box, whose wrapper was not positioned, and widened the page by 13 px at 1024; the `Table` wrapper is now `relative` (tested).

FY-9. **"Visits booked", and today drawn as still under way (finding 5; amends FX-32, F57A-21).** — The report counts visits scheduled in the period in any status (B57-32), cancelled ones and today's still-expected visitors included; the admin dashboard's "Visits today" leaves cancelled visits out (B57-31). One definition for both would be a backend change, so the report says what it counts:
   - The tile is "Visits booked" with "Incl. 14 cancelled · 37 walk-ins"; the footnote says booked visits include cancelled ones and today's visitors still expected; the chart is "Visits booked per day".
   - `fillDailySeries(…, today)` marks today `partial`, labelled "Tue, 6 Oct 2026 (today, so far: includes visits still expected)" in the readout, the data table and the mark's name. `ColumnChart` draws it with a hatched pattern (a column) or a hollow point (the line), with a legend line under the plot: texture and words, not colour alone. The value axis still includes it; the direct peak label goes to the busiest complete day.
   - `dailySummaryText` (pure, tested): "339 visits over 30 days. The busiest full day was Sun, 20 Sep 2026, with 22 visits. Today so far: 27 visits, including visits still expected." An unfinished today is never "the busiest day".

FY-10. **The printed report (finding 11).** — `PrintableReportHeader` (paper only, `aria-hidden`, like the evacuation list's): company, "Visitor report", the period and its length, the time zone, when the figures were generated (`dataUpdatedAt`) and who printed them; the screen header is `print:hidden`. The five tiles are one row on paper (`print:grid-cols-5`; the "odd last tile spans two" rule is `not-print:` only). Charts redraw at `PRINT_CHART_WIDTH` (620 px) on `beforeprint` (synchronously, `flushSync`, because the browser lays the page out for paper right after the event) and stretch to the paper with `print:w-full`; `afterprint` restores the screen width. The app shell's background is white in print. Chart cards already avoid page breaks. Verified as an A4 PDF with background graphics (the probe dispatched `beforeprint`, which `page.pdf()` does not): page 1 holds the header, the tile row, the daily chart and the department chart; page 2 the hourly chart and top hosts; no slate background.

## Kit

FY-11. **Checkboxes and list radios are 24 px on every pointer (finding 10).** — `Checkbox` and `RadioGroup` (list) inputs are `size-6`, centred on the label's first line (`-my-0.5`), instead of 20 px with 24 px only on coarse pointers. A 24 px target passes WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 whatever its neighbours. The reviewer's failing layout could not be reproduced exactly (at 390 px with a fine pointer the old box also passed axe without a camera and with a two-camera picker), so `e2e/reception-axe-narrow.spec.ts` checks both: no serious or critical axe violation on the Scan tab at 390 px with a fine pointer, and a beep checkbox of at least 24 × 24 px (fails on the old box). Settings, departments, user roles, the cancel dialog and the invite form's pass-delivery radios were looked at in Chromium after the change: aligned.
